What are the fire safety responsibilities for high-rise buildings?

14:02 PM
bolt
Correction Hint Updated 2 minutes ago based on 2024 Amendments

Under current national regulations, fire safety responsibilities for high-rise buildings are categorized into four critical operation domains:

01. Evacuation

Maintenance of smoke-proof stairwells and emergency lighting systems.

02. Suppression

Routine inspection of indoor hydrants and automatic sprinkler manifolds.
